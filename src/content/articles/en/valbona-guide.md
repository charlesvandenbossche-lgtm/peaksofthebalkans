Valbonë is not one village but a string of hamlets along the Valbona river, inside the Valbona Valley National Park (about 8,000 hectares, protected since 1996). Most Peaks of the Balkans hikers arrive here over the pass from Theth or by the Komani Lake ferry, sleep one night and move on. With a spare day, the valley rewards you with easy walks, cold river pools and a view of the highest peaks of the Albanian Alps.

## Valbonë at a glance

| What | Time / distance | Level | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kukaj viewpoint and old mill | about 9 km, 4 h return, ± 675 m climb | easy to moderate | Small hamlet about 3 km from Valbonë; also reachable by 4x4 |
| Snake Meadows (Livadhet e Gjarpërit) | about 10 km loop, 4 h, ± 540 m climb | easy to moderate | Meadows at about 1,200 m with an agritourism guesthouse |
| Rragam and the Valbona waterfall | from the end of the road, 2–3 h | easy | Track runs through a mostly dry riverbed; the last hamlet of the valley |
| River pools near the Shoshan canyon | at the valley entrance | easy | Cold swimming spots below the bridge; reach them by car or minibus |
| Dragobi cave (Shpella e Dragobisë) | short walk from Dragobi | easy | Where the national hero Bajram Curri died in 1925 |
| Maja e Rosit (2,522 m) | about 18 km, 10 h, ± 1,590 m climb | hard | Long summit day; shorter if you sleep in Kukaj |

Walking times come from local hiking guides and are rough: allow extra time for photos and breaks.

## Easy day hikes from Valbonë

- **Kukaj.** The classic rest-day walk. You climb out of the valley to a tiny hamlet with a viewpoint over the Valbona valley and an old water mill on the way. Guesthouse Rosi in Kukaj is only accessible on foot or by 4x4, so it is a good place to stop for a coffee or stay a night if you want to continue to Maja e Rosit.
- **Snake Meadows.** A loop to the high pastures of Livadhet e Gjarpërit, at about 1,200 m. A 4x4 track leads up to the Arif Kadria guesthouse, whose owners can collect guests from the road for a fee.
- **Rragam and the waterfall.** The flattest option. From the end of the asphalt at Fushë e Gjesë, follow the stony riverbed up to Rragam, the last settlement before the Valbona Pass. Beyond it is the Valbona (Rragam) waterfall. This is also the first or last part of [the Theth to Valbonë hike](/en/theth-to-valbona/).

## Swimming, caves and the lower valley

The Valbona river is ice-cold but clear. The best-known pools are near the **Shoshan canyon** at the entrance of the valley, the first thing you see when you drive in from Bajram Curri. About 3 km downstream from Valbonë lies the small **Xhema lake** with an old mill, reached by a narrow footbridge; paths around it are not well marked.

On the south side of the valley, the hamlet of **Dragobi** has the **Dragobi cave**, where Bajram Curri, after whom the regional town is named, hid and died in 1925. It is a short, easy outing with a historic story.

## Longer hikes for strong walkers

- **Maja e Rosit (2,522 m).** About 10 hours from the valley, with almost 1,600 m of climbing. Start from Kukaj to make it shorter.
- **Towards Çerem via the Kollata massif.** About 16 km and 7 hours. This is the next Peaks of the Balkans stage, so only do it as a day hike if you are not walking the full loop. See [the Peaks of the Balkans stages](/en/peaks-of-the-balkans-stages/).

## Practical tips

- **Season.** Most guesthouses open from about May or June to late September or October and close for the winter, when snow blocks the high paths.
- **Where to sleep.** The valley has dozens of family guesthouses; half board is the norm. Recent reports put prices at about €20–35 per person per night with dinner and breakfast (2025). Book ahead for July and August. More in [where to sleep on the Peaks of the Balkans](/en/peaks-of-the-balkans-accommodation/).
- **Cash and signal.** There are no ATMs or banks in the valley. Bring lek (or euros) from Shkodër or Bajram Curri. Mobile reception is patchy outside the centre.
- **Getting there.** Valbonë is about 25–35 km from Bajram Curri, roughly an hour by road. A minibus has traditionally left Bajram Curri in the afternoon and returned early in the morning; ask your guesthouse for the current times. In summer, minibuses also meet the [Komani Lake ferry](/en/komani-lake-ferry/) at Fierzë.

If you would rather not organise permits, transfers and guesthouses yourself, [The Balkan Trail](https://thebalkantrail.com) takes care of them while you walk at your own pace, with guides along the route and optional luggage transport and gear rental.
