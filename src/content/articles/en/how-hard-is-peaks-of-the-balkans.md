The trail itself is not dangerous, but it is long and the days add up. The difficulty comes from the cumulative climbing, heat in summer, navigation in some sections and the remoteness.

## Difficulty per stage

| Stage | Difficulty | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Theth → Valbonë | Hard | ± 1,000 m up to the Valbona Pass, steep descent |
| 2 Valbonë → Çerem | Moderate | Long, but gradual |
| 3 Çerem → Dobërdol | Moderate | Steady climb to the plateau |
| 4 Dobërdol → Milishevc | Moderate–hard | High ridge, exposed to weather |
| 5 Milishevc → Rekë e Allagës | Moderate | Long descent |
| 6 Rekë e Allagës → Drelaj | Easy–moderate | Shorter day |
| 7 Drelaj → Babino Polje | Hard | Long, 18–22 km |
| 8 Babino Polje → Plav | Moderate | Hrid Lake, long descent to Plav |
| 9 Plav → Vusanje | Moderate–hard | Long, with climbs |
| 10 Vusanje → Theth | Hard | Peja Pass and steep descent to Theth |

## What makes it hard

- **Climbing every day.** Almost every stage has 800–1,200 m of ascent and as much descent.
- **Heat.** July and August can be over 30 °C in the valleys.
- **Navigation.** Good waymarking in Albania and Montenegro; gaps in Kosovo. Use a GPX track.
- **Remoteness.** Few shops, limited phone signal, and help can be hours away.
- **Livestock guard dogs** on the high pastures. See [safety](/en/peaks-of-the-balkans-safety/).

## What is easier than people think

- **No technical terrain:** no ladders, chains or exposed scrambling.
- **You don't carry food for days:** guesthouses provide dinner, breakfast and packed lunches.
- **Flexible:** you can skip sections with local transfers.

## Who can do it?

| You… | Full loop in 10 days? |
|---|---|
| Regularly hike 20 km with 1,000 m of climbing | Yes |
| Do day hikes but no multi-day treks yet | Yes, with training; consider 5–7 days of highlights |
| Have little hiking experience | Choose the highlights with support |
| Have knee problems | Bring poles; the descents are long |

## Making it easier

A light daypack instead of a full pack makes the biggest difference. With [The Balkan Trail](https://thebalkantrail.com), you can add luggage transport so your main bag travels by car or horse, and you walk at your own pace with guides ahead and behind you on the route.
