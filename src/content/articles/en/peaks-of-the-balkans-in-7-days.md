The full Peaks of the Balkans loop is about 192 km and usually takes 10 days. Most people do not have that much holiday, and the good news is that the trail shortens well. By leaving out the Kosovo section you keep the big passes, the high plateaus and the Ropojana valley, and you still finish where you started, in Theth.

## The 7-day itinerary at a glance

This is the route most hikers and several tour operators use for a one-week trek, walked anticlockwise from Theth. Distances are approximate and differ between sources by 10–20 %.

| Day | Stage | About | Highlight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theth → Valbonë | 15–18 km, ± 1,000 m up | Valbona Pass (about 1,760 m), the classic day |
| 2 | Valbonë → Çerem | about 16 km, ± 1,150 m up | Prosllopi Pass, quiet forest and pastures; little water on the way |
| 3 | Çerem → Dobërdol | 13–16 km | The wide Dobërdol plateau and its summer shepherd huts |
| 4 | Dobërdol → Babino Polje | long day | Three-border point (Albania, Kosovo, Montenegro), down into Montenegro |
| 5 | Babino Polje → Plav | 16–21 km | Hrid Lake, the only town (shops and ATM) |
| 6 | Plav → Vusanje | 16–22 km | Bor ridge and the Grebaje side valleys |
| 7 | Vusanje → Theth | 17–21 km | Ropojana valley, Peja Pass, steep descent to Theth |

Add one day before (getting from Shkodër to Theth) and one day after (back to Shkodër or on to the airport), so plan on about nine days from home to home.

## What you miss by skipping Kosovo

Leaving out Kosovo removes the stages through Milishevc, Rekë e Allagës and Drelaj in the Rugova highlands. You lose the quieter, less touristy villages and the easy option of climbing Gjeravica from Dobërdol. In return, you skip the section where waymarking is least consistent, and you only need border permits for Albania and Montenegro instead of three countries. Read more in [the permits guide](/en/peaks-of-the-balkans-permits/).

## Only 5 or 6 hiking days?

With less time, shorten the route further with a car transfer:

- **6 days:** walk days 1–4 as above, then take a transfer from Babino Polje or Plav to Vusanje (or to Grlja and the Ropojana valley) and walk back over the Peja Pass to Theth. You miss Hrid Lake and the Plav–Vusanje stage.
- **5 days:** some operators walk Theth → Valbonë → Çerem → Dobërdol → Babino Polje, then drive to the Ropojana valley for the last day back to Theth.

Compare all ten stages in [the stage-by-stage guide](/en/peaks-of-the-balkans-stages/).

## Is 7 days hard?

Yes, but it is doable for fit hikers. You walk six to eight hours a day with up to about 1,000–1,200 m of climbing, without a rest day. Theth–Valbonë, Dobërdol–Babino Polje and Vusanje–Theth are the toughest. If you are not sure about your fitness, read [how hard the Peaks of the Balkans is](/en/how-hard-is-peaks-of-the-balkans/) first.

## Practical tips for a one-week trek

- **Season.** The high passes are usually snow-free from about mid-June to late September. In 2026 the Theth–Valbonë summer path stayed closed until late May because of heavy snow.
- **Permits.** Apply for the Albania and Montenegro border permits at least a week in advance, even if you skip Kosovo.
- **Cash.** Plav is the only place on this route with an ATM. Bring enough lek and euros for the first days.
- **Book ahead.** In July and August guesthouses in Theth, Valbonë and Dobërdol fill up. Half board is the norm.
- **Getting to Theth.** Minibuses run from Shkodër in summer; see [how to get to Theth](/en/getting-to-theth/).

If you would rather not juggle permits, transfers and guesthouse bookings in a single week, [The Balkan Trail](https://thebalkantrail.com) arranges them for you, so you walk at your own pace with guides along the route and optional luggage transport and gear rental.
