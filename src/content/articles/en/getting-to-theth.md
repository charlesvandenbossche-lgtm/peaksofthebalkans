The Peaks of the Balkans is a loop, so where you start depends on where you fly in. Theth is the classic start, but it is not the only option.

## Airports

| Airport | Best for | Distance to trail |
|---|---|---|
| Tirana (TIA), Albania | Theth, Valbonë | ± 4–5 h to Theth via Shkodër |
| Podgorica (TGD), Montenegro | Plav, Vusanje, or Theth via Shkodër | ± 3 h to Plav |
| Pristina (PRN), Kosovo | Drelaj, Rekë e Allagës via Peja | ± 2 h to Peja |

## Shkodër → Theth by minibus

- **Departures:** usually around 07:00 from Shkodër, with a second run in the afternoon in high season (around 14:00 in 2026).
- **Time:** 2–3 hours; the road was asphalted to Theth in recent years.
- **Price:** about €10 per person (2026).
- **Booking:** ask your guesthouse in Shkodër or Theth; most can reserve a seat and pick you up at your hotel.

## Komani Lake ferry → Valbonë

The ferry over Komani Lake is one of the most scenic boat trips in the Balkans, and the classic way to reach Valbonë.

| | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Departure Koman | 09:00 |
| Departure Fierzë | 13:00 |
| Crossing time | ± 2.5 h |
| Price | about €9 online, €10 cash |

From Shkodër, a minibus brings you to the Koman pier early in the morning; at Fierzë, minibuses wait to drive you to Valbonë. Times are seasonal: check them before you travel.

## Starting from Montenegro or Kosovo

- **Plav / Vusanje:** bus from Podgorica to Plav (around 3 hours), then taxi or local transfer to Vusanje.
- **Peja / Rugova:** bus from Pristina to Peja, then taxi to Drelaj or Rekë e Allagës.

## Getting back

On a loop, you finish where you started. If you walk only part of the route, arrange a transfer back in advance: taxis between valleys can be expensive because roads go all the way around the mountains. [The Balkan Trail](https://thebalkantrail.com) includes the local transfers during the trek and offers transfers from and to Tirana.
