## Popularity keeps growing

The Theth – Valbonë stage has become one of the most walked mountain paths in the Western Balkans. Figures from the GIZ-supported tourism programme counted more than 43,000 hikers on that section between July and November 2024. The rest of the loop remains much quieter.

## What to know for 2027

1. **Book early for July and August**, especially in Theth, Valbonë and Vusanje.
2. **Permits** still need to be requested for all three countries; allow 15 days or more.
3. **June** remains a gamble in heavy-snow years: ask locally before you go.
4. **Cash** remains essential: card payments are still rare in the mountains.

## Planning 2027 without the hassle

Organised departures for 2027 are already open. [The Balkan Trail](https://thebalkantrail.com) runs from June to September, with permits and transfers included.
