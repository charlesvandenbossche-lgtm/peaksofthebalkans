Distances and times differ between guidebooks, signposts and GPS tracks, sometimes by 10–20 %. The figures below are approximate ranges for the standard route, walked anticlockwise from Theth. Plan with your own GPX track and add time for breaks and photos.

## Overview of all stages

| # | Stage | Distance | Time | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theth → Valbonë | 14–17 km | 6–7 h | Steep climb to the Valbona Pass (± 1,800 m), long descent |
| 2 | Valbonë → Çerem | 14–16 km | 6–7 h | Forest and pastures, quiet border area |
| 3 | Çerem → Dobërdol | 13–15 km | 5–6 h | Up to the Dobërdol plateau and its summer huts |
| 4 | Dobërdol → Milishevc | 13–15 km | 5–6 h | Into Kosovo along the border ridge; Gjeravica nearby |
| 5 | Milishevc → Rekë e Allagës | 15–19 km | 6–7 h | High pastures, then down towards the Rugova area |
| 6 | Rekë e Allagës → Drelaj | 13–15 km | 5–6 h | Rugova highlands and villages |
| 7 | Drelaj → Babino Polje | 18–22 km | 7–8 h | Long day; into Montenegro over Hajla's flanks |
| 8 | Babino Polje → Plav | 16–21 km | 6–7 h | Via Hrid Lake, one of the prettiest spots on the trail |
| 9 | Plav → Vusanje | 16–22 km | 6–8 h | Via Bor peak and the Grebaje side valleys |
| 10 | Vusanje → Theth | 17–21 km | 7–8 h | Ropojana valley, Peja Pass (± 1,700 m), steep descent to Theth |
| | **Total** | **± 192 km** | **10 days** | **± 9,800 m of ascent** |

## Stage 1: Theth → Valbonë

The most famous day and the busiest. From Theth you climb about 1,000 m through beech forest to the Valbona Pass, then descend into the Valbonë valley. There is a café hut near the pass in summer. In early summer snow can block the pass; in 2026 the summer path was closed until late May because of heavy snow. Read the [full stage guide](/en/theth-to-valbona/).

## Stages 2–3: Valbonë → Çerem → Dobërdol

Quieter days near the Albania–Kosovo border. Çerem is a small summer settlement; Dobërdol is a wide plateau where shepherds spend the summer and offer simple beds and meals.

## Stages 4–6: through Kosovo

You cross into Kosovo on the high ridge near the three-country point. Waymarking in Kosovo is less consistent than elsewhere, so a GPX track matters most here. Milishevc, Rekë e Allagës and Drelaj have guesthouses; Peja is a short drive away if you need a town.

## Stages 7–9: Montenegro

Stage 7 is one of the longest days. Stage 8 passes Hrid Lake (Hridsko jezero), a glacial lake surrounded by pines. Plav is the only real town on the route, with shops and an ATM. Stage 9 takes you to Vusanje, the gateway to the Grlja waterfall and the Ropojana valley.

## Stage 10: Vusanje → Theth

Through the Ropojana valley, back into Albania over the Peja Pass and down the steep path to Theth. Long, but one of the most spectacular days.

## Shortening the route

If you have 5–7 days, the most popular choice is to walk stages 1, 3, 4, 8 and 10 and use local transfers in between. That is roughly what [The Balkan Trail](https://thebalkantrail.com) does in its five hiking days, with the permits and transfers arranged. See also [guided or self-guided](/en/guided-or-self-guided-peaks-of-the-balkans/).
